# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary fixes the words used for buying a course and getting money back, so that what the learner sees, what the team says and what the code says all mean the same thing.

## Language

### Buying

**Learner**:
The person who takes a course.
_Avoid_: Student, customer

**Purchase**:
One learner buying one course once, or a team buying a licence for a course. The amount paid is fixed at the moment of purchase.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

### Getting money back

**Refund**:
The amount paid on a Purchase, returned to the learner. A Refund ends that Purchase and the learner's access to the course.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund window**:
The fourteen calendar days after a Purchase in which the learner may make a Refund request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refund request**:
A learner's ask for a Refund on one Purchase, with their reason, waiting for a person on the team to approve or decline it.
_Avoid_: Pending chargeback, cancellation request, reversal request

**Requested**:
The state of a Refund request that nobody on the team has decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, open

**Approved**:
The state of a Refund request the team has accepted, so the Refund is made.

**Declined**:
The state of a Refund request the team has turned down; no money is returned and the Purchase stands.
_Avoid_: Rejected, denied

**Chargeback**:
A dispute the cardholder raises through their bank, outside Cadence. It is not part of the Refund flow and must never be used to describe a Refund request.
